Matcha, not coffee

"Matcha" is two Japanese characters combined: 抹 (matsu), which means "to grind into powder," and 茶 (cha), which just means "tea”. So saying “I’d like one matcha tea, please”, is a bit redundant (as is "matcha powder").

I often crave coffee, but if I have it on a workday, or on a busy weekend morning, I crash by the afternoon and cannot focus on anything.

So, a few months ago I started swapping coffee for matcha. It took me a while to get used to it, but now it’s becoming something I look forward to in the afternoons.

How I make mine

  • 1 tsp matcha
  • a mug's worth of oat milk (I think oat milk pairs better with matcha than cow's milk)
  • a dab of vanilla bean paste
  • sugar, however much you like (honey doens't work)

That's it. A cozy, warm drink with no crash 🍵.
